# A/F/B/B2 comparison figures

**Figure 1. Detection performance on unseen generators.** AUROC for A, F, B and B2 on Glide, Midjourney and Wukong. Each generator evaluation includes 2,500 AI images and the same 1,166 real images. Dots show the three seeds; diamonds and whiskers show their mean and sample SD.

**Figure 2. Average performance and cross-generator consistency.** For each seed, Mean AUROC and population SD are calculated across the three generators. Diamonds average these statistics over seeds 42, 123 and 2026; whiskers show sample SD across seeds, not confidence intervals.

B2 is the frozen-frequency fusion variant. The original A/F/B figures are retained.
